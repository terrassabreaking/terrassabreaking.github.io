Breaking classes in Terrassa

Learn breaking (breakdance) in Terrassa, near Barcelona. Classes for kids, teens and adults every Friday at the Casal Cívic de Sant Pere. No experience needed, and your first class is free.

Book a free trial class
When
Fridays, 5:30 to 6:30 pm
Where
Casal Cívic de Sant Pere, Passeig 22 de Juliol, 337. Open in maps
Price
€20 per month + €10 per year membership of the Sant Pere Neighbourhood Association. First class free.

What is breaking?

Breaking is a street dance born in the Bronx, New York, in the 1970s, and one of the original elements of hip hop culture. It mixes rhythm, acrobatics and creativity, and debuted as an Olympic discipline at Paris 2024.

Who is it for?

Kids from 5, teens and adults. Our current group ranges from 9 to over 70 years old. Classes are taught in Catalan and Spanish.

What you'll learn

Breaking foundations (toprock, footwork and freezes), more advanced moves as you progress, and the history and values of hip hop culture.

Teachers

Classes are taught by bboy Jan and bboy Toolz, dancers from Terrassa Breaking with years of experience in the scene.

Already dancing?

Experienced breakers are welcome to train with us on Fridays. Send us a DM on Instagram first and we'll share the times.

FAQ

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, trainers and a water bottle.

How do I sign up?

Fill in the trial class form, DM us on Instagram, or come by the casal on a Friday at 5:30 pm.
