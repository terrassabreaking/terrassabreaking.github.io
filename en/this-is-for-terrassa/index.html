This is for Terrassa

Vol. 6

Saturday, October 24, 2026
Crew vs crew breaking battle
Casal Cívic de Sant Pere, Terrassa

Register your crew

€5 per person. 16 spots. Registration closes October 16.

Every autumn since 2021, the Casal Cívic de Sant Pere in Terrassa, 30 minutes from Barcelona, hosts one of the area's longest-running crew vs crew battles. Crews come from Barcelona, Zaragoza, Madrid, Valencia and France, with live rap and graffiti battles. Up to 20 crews have entered a single edition.

Prizes

500 €

A trip (travel and accommodation) to compete at an international breaking event.

250 €

Snipes gift card for second place.

Format

Crews of at least 5 members. Battles are timed and get longer each round.

10 min

Come and watch

Free entry. If you're not dancing, we recommend arriving from 7 to 8 pm for the most intense battles. Drinks available at the bar.

Getting here

Casal Cívic de Sant Pere, Passeig 22 de Juliol, 337, Terrassa. From Barcelona, take the Renfe R4 or FGC S1 to Terrassa Nord: the venue is next to the station. Open in maps
